Our Streaming Topic

Our group chose entertainment gaming as our main streaming concept. We wanted something that could keep viewers entertained while also giving us opportunities to interact with them.

Why Gaming?

Gaming gives us many opportunities to create fun, active, and engaging content. Instead of only presenting information, we can interact with viewers while the stream is happening.

We believe entertainment gaming can make the audience feel more involved because they can watch, react, communicate, and participate.

🎮

Our Goal

To create a stream that is fun, interactive, entertaining, and easy for our audience to connect with.

02

Our Target Audience

Understanding our audience helped us decide what type of content would work best for our stream.

🎮

Gamers

👥

Young Viewers

💬

Interactive Viewers

02

03

Entertainment

The content should keep viewers interested and encourage them to continue watching.

04

Interaction

Viewers should have opportunities to communicate and feel included in the stream.

Our Research Journey

Our streaming idea developed gradually through each Experience Guide. Every activity helped us understand another part of the project.

EG 1

Introduction to Streaming

We first learned what streaming is and explored how people use streaming to share content.

EG 2

Streaming Platforms and JavaScript Operators

We explored streaming platforms and learned about JavaScript operators as part of our ICT activities.

EG 3-4

Audience Analysis of Streaming Content

We analyzed streaming audiences and considered who would be interested in our content.

EG 5-6

Viewer Needs and Characteristics of Good Streams

We identified what viewers need and what qualities can make a stream effective and enjoyable.

EG 7-8

Documentation of Streaming Needs

We documented our findings and used them to organize our streaming concept.

What We Learned

Our group’s idea did not come together all at once. At first, we had different opinions about what type of content to choose, which made the process a little difficult.

Instead of picking the first idea we liked, we compared our choices using what we learned from the Experience Guides. Our discussions eventually led us to entertainment gaming because it gives us more chances to interact with viewers while keeping the content fun and active.

Looking back at EG 1 to EG 8, we can see that our idea changed as we learned more. What started as a simple thought became a more planned concept because of our research, discussions, and teamwork.
